# Further Reading — Real-World Engineering Case Studies

Curated pointers to real engineering write-ups, organized by which module
they reinforce. These are supplementary — read them *after* the matching
module, once you have the theory to recognize what's actually happening.
Most of these are indexed (with links to the original engineering blogs)
via [ByteByteGo's system-design-101](https://github.com/ByteByteGoHq/system-design-101)
repo, which is a good general-purpose index of "how it works" writeups to
revisit over time.

Do not treat this list as required reading before quizzes — it's for
deepening intuition once a module is done, or for browsing when you want a
break from theory but want to stay sharp.
